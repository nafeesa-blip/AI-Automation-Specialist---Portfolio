How a lead moves through an automated workflow.

Capture

A form, webhook or API call brings the lead in and logs it automatically.

Clean and enrich

Duplicates are checked, data is cleaned and company details are added with AI.

Score and route

An LLM scores each lead from 0 to 100 and sends hot ones to the right person with an alert.

Follow up

Personalized emails go out on a schedule and stop when the lead replies. Everything is logged in the CRM.

    Webinar lead automation: round-robin assignment & reminders

    n8n Automation Developer

    Webinar sign-ups were handled manually, so leads were missed and follow-ups came late. I built an n8n workflow that captures leads from a website form, logs them in a Google Sheets tracker, flags duplicates for manual review and assigns advisors by round-robin. Each lead gets an instant welcome message, the advisor gets an alert, and automatic reminders go out 24 hours and 1 hour before the webinar. The tracker updates itself at every step. The result is faster response, even lead distribution and fewer no-shows. This is a sample project built with demo data.

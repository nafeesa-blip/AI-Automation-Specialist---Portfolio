Webinar lead automation: round-robin assignment & reminders

n8n Automation Developer

Webinar sign-ups were handled manually, so leads were missed and follow-ups came late. I built an n8n workflow that captures leads from a website form, logs them in a Google Sheets tracker, flags duplicates for manual review and assigns advisors by round-robin. Each lead gets an instant welcome message, the advisor gets an alert, and automatic reminders go out 24 hours and 1 hour before the webinar. The tracker updates itself at every step. The result is faster response, even lead distribution and fewer no-shows. Demonstration workflow built with demo data.

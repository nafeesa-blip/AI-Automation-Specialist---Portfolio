Process

My SEO loop

  1. Audit what Google seesA crawl with JavaScript rendering on and off, Search Console indexing, page speed on mobile and structured data. AI-built sites often look perfect to people and empty to Google.
  2. Fix the foundationPrerendered HTML for crawlers, clean URLs with canonicals, unique titles and meta descriptions, Product schema, sitemap and robots.txt.
  3. Get products into Google ShoppingA Merchant Center feed built from your own product data, refreshed daily, with titles, GTINs, images and shipping set up the way Google wants.
  4. Map keywords to pagesOne search intent per page: collections, products and buying guides, with internal links between them.
  5. Track leads, not just rankingsGA4 events for forms, carts and orders, split by organic search and Shopping, in one dashboard you own.
  6. Report and pushMonthly report, then the next push on keywords sitting just below page 1.

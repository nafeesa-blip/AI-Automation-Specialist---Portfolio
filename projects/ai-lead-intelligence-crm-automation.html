The challenge

Finding the right B2B prospects usually means hours of manual searching, copying company details into spreadsheets, checking emails one by one and writing every first message by hand. Records get duplicated, data goes stale and good leads slip through the cracks.

The solution

I built an n8n pipeline that runs every morning on its own. It turns the target criteria into search queries, finds matching companies, verifies contacts, enriches and scores every record with AI, and only then pushes qualified leads into the CRM, with a personalized email already written.

How it works

  1. Schedule triggerThe workflow starts automatically every day at 9:00 AM.
  2. Generate search queriesOpenAI turns the ideal customer profile into targeted company search queries.
  3. Search companiesThe Apollo.io API returns companies that match each query.
  4. Get contact detailsThe Hunter.io API finds decision makers and verified email addresses.
  5. Enrich and cleanAI adds company context (industry, size, focus), duplicates are checked against Google Sheets and the data is standardized.
  6. Qualify with AIEach lead gets a score from 0 to 100. Leads scoring 70 or more continue; the rest move to a nurture list.
  7. Sync and reach outQualified leads are created or updated in HubSpot, logged in Google Sheets, and a personalized outreach email is sent through Gmail.

Key features

  • Fully scheduled, no manual trigger needed
  • Verified emails only (Hunter.io)
  • AI enrichment and lead scoring with a clear threshold
  • Duplicate protection before anything reaches the CRM
  • Personalized outreach written per lead
  • Nurture routing so lower-score leads are not lost

Results

  • Prospecting runs every day without manual searching
  • Only verified, deduplicated and scored leads reach HubSpot
  • Sales starts each conversation with context and a ready first draft
  • A clean Google Sheets log of every lead and message

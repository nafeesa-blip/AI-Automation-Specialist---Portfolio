Build walkthrough

From one supplier promotion to a bilingual campaign, step by step

n8n · OpenAI · Airtable · Mailchimp · Creatomate

A tour operator sent a sanitized supplier promotion (a Spanish Word document) and asked to see the full process. This page follows that one document through the system: what the AI extracts, which safeguards run, the English and Spanish copy, the graphics, carousel and video, the Mailchimp email, the approval step and the learning loop.

The system

Three n8n workflows

These are screenshots of the real workflows running in n8n. Workflow 1 turns a supplier document into a draft campaign. Workflow 2 runs every minute and only lets approved content through. Workflow 3 runs weekly and feeds what worked back into the next campaign.

Workflow 1: supplier promotion → draft campaign. If a safeguard blocks the promotion, the run stops at “Needs a person” and nothing is generated.
Workflow 2: approval guard. Expires old offers, sends edited content back to review, has the AI revise on request, and schedules only approved content. Workflow 3: learning loop. Pulls results every Monday and saves insights that the next campaign’s AI writer uses.

Step 1 · Input

The supplier document

The promotion arrives as a Word document in Spanish. The supplier is fictional and the client marked the file as test data. The agency commission and net rate values are hidden on this page.

The original document. Green: extracted and verified. Amber: the May 16 departure, which is on request and not part of the promotion. Red: agency-only data that must never reach public content.
The upload form generated by n8n.

What makes this document tricky

  • Three departures are listed, but only two are promotional. May 16 is “on request” and must not be advertised with the discount.
  • The discount applies only to the second passenger sharing a double room, never to single supplements.
  • The booking window is short: November 18 to 30, 2026.
  • The agency commission and an internal net rate are in the same document as the public offer.
  • The price table becomes loose text once it leaves Word, so dates and prices have to be paired correctly.

Step 3 · Safeguards

Checks the AI cannot skip

The safeguards are plain code, so they give the same answer every time. Below is their real output on this document (run on Nov 19, 2026). The promotion is marked Needs attention because one departure is on request.

  • Check
    ON_REQUESTDeparture 2027-05-16 is "on request" with the supplier. Confirm availability before advertising it.
  • Info
    NOT_PROMOTIONAL2027-05-16 is in the price table but is NOT a promotional departure. It will not be advertised with the discount.
  • Info
    EXAMPLE_MATCHESSupplier's worked example (1756) matches our calculation for 2027-03-14.
  • Info
    CONFIDENTIAL_FOUNDAgency-only data found (commission ■■%, 1 net rate(s)). It is stored separately and blocked from all public content.
  • Info
    TEST_DOCUMENTSupplier marked this document as a test / simulation.

Discount recalculated, never copied

The system computes the second-passenger price itself and compares it with the supplier’s worked example. For April 18 the supplier gave no example; this figure comes only from the calculation.

What happens when the AI gets something wrong

The same document, with four mistakes an AI model can make: two digits of a price swapped (2,195 → 2,159), the on-request May departure marked promotional, an invented deadline, and a wrong worked example. Result: Blocked, and nothing is generated.

  • Blocked
    PRICE_NOT_IN_DOCPrice 2159 for 2027-03-14 does not appear in the supplier document.
  • Blocked
    PROMO_DATE_NOT_LISTED2027-05-16 is marked as promotional, but the supplier's list of promotional departures does not include it.
  • Blocked
    DEADLINE_NOT_IN_DOCBooking deadline 2026-12-15 not found in the supplier document.
  • Blocked
    EXAMPLE_MISMATCHSupplier's worked example (1800) does not match 20% off any promotional departure. Ask the supplier.

Confidential data leak scan

Every caption, slide, script and email is scanned before approval, and again just before publishing. The generated campaign below: no leaks found. A test sentence that mentions the commission and net rate is caught on 4 terms: hidden value, hidden value, commission, net rate.

Step 7 · Human approval

Nothing goes out until a person approves it

Every item lands in an Airtable Interface next to the original supplier file and the safeguard flags. The reviewer can edit any text, ask for changes in plain words or approve. Workflow 2 checks the dashboard every minute.

  1. Pending approvalThe promotion, its departures and every post, carousel slide, video and email, with the source document next to them.
  2. Changes requestedThe reviewer writes a comment such as “make the Facebook post shorter”. The AI rewrites only that item and sends it back to review.
  3. ApprovedThe leak scan and date checks run once more. The content is marked Scheduled and the Mailchimp test email is sent.
  4. Edited after approvalA fingerprint of the approved text is stored. If anyone changes it later, the item goes back to review automatically.
  5. Expired or correctedAfter the booking deadline, or when the supplier sends a correction, the scheduled content is held.
Step 8 · Mailchimp

The email campaign and the data behind it

The English email as built for Mailchimp. The Spanish version is built from the same template.

Where each part of the email comes from:

Subject and previewAI copy: “Spain & Portugal 2027: 20% off your travel companion” / “12 days, 6 cities, from USD 2,195. Book by November 30.”
Hero imageThe single graphic from Step 5, same language
Price tableCalculated by the safeguards in Step 3, not written by the AI
Departures listedPromotional only (Mar 14, Apr 18). May 16 is excluded
TermsRestrictions from the document, written out in full
FooterMailchimp merge tags for the address and unsubscribe link
AudienceThe client’s Mailchimp audience. Sending each language only to its own subscribers (by a language field) is set up on the client’s list

The API calls n8n makes:

POST /3.0/campaigns
{
  "type": "regular",
  "recipients": { "list_id": "<audience>" },
  "settings": {
    "title": "Spain & Portugal 2027 – 2nd Passenger 20% Off (EN)",
    "subject_line": "Spain & Portugal 2027: 20% off your travel companion",
    "preview_text": "12 days, 6 cities, from USD 2,195. Book by November 30.",
    "from_name": "Demo Travel Co.",
    "reply_to": "<team inbox>"
  }
}
PUT /3.0/campaigns/{id}/content   { "html": "<the email below>" }
POST /3.0/campaigns/{id}/actions/test   (after approval)

Both campaigns are created as drafts. On approval a test email goes to the team. Scheduling the send to the full audience needs a paid Mailchimp plan, so it is part of the client build.

Step 9 · Learning loop

Results feed the next campaign

Every Monday, workflow 3 collects results into a Performance table, asks the AI what drives clicks and enquiries, and saves the answer as an active Insight. The next time the AI writes a campaign, it receives that insight and lists how it applied it.

Custom Windows invoice generator with PDF export

A simple Windows invoice generator, designed to create professional invoices from one consistent custom template, with automatic totals, numbering and PDF export, and no subscription software.

The challenge

Small businesses often pay monthly for accounting software just to send invoices, or they edit Word files by hand and make mistakes with totals, numbers and dates.

The solution

I designed a lightweight Windows app built in Python that keeps one professional template, does the calculations automatically and exports a clean PDF, with a local history of every invoice.

How it works

  1. Enter detailsThe user enters client, items, quantities and prices in a friendly form.
  2. Automatic calculationsPython calculates totals, taxes, invoice number and dates.
  3. Generate PDFThe invoice is rendered from the custom template as a PDF.
  4. Save, download or printThe invoice is saved to a local history and can be printed or shared.

Key features

  • Manual invoice entry with a friendly interface
  • Automatic totals, numbering and dates
  • Payment status tracking
  • PDF generation from one consistent template
  • Local invoice history
  • No subscription-based accounting software

Results

  • Consistent, professional invoices every time
  • Fewer manual calculation errors
  • No recurring software costs

Audit: what was holding this listing back

ElementIssue foundFix
TitleComma-separated keyword list; “candle” repeated 5 times; the scent isn’t mentionedScent first (what people search), then type, size, jar, gift angle and the wick
TagsOnly 6 of 13 used; single words like “soy” and “gift” are too broadAll 13 tags, each a real 2–3 word search phrase, no repeats of the title order
AttributesScent, holiday and occasion left blankAll attributes filled (scent: lavender, occasion: birthday, housewarming)
First photoDark photo on a cluttered shelfBright, natural-light photo with the lit candle and wood wick, square crop
DescriptionStarts with shipping policyScent notes, burn time, size and care in the first 2 lines, then policy

Photo plan

  1. 1. HeroLit candle, wood wick crackle, soft daylight, linen background.
  2. 2. Scent notesLavender sprigs and cedar wood with three scent notes in text.
  3. 3. SizeCandle in a hand for scale, with “8 oz · 45-hour burn”.
  4. 4. Gift readyKraft box, ribbon and a handwritten note card option.
  5. 5. Video5-second clip of lighting the wick (Etsy shows video in search).

Etsy Ads and testing

  • Small daily Etsy Ads budget on the rewritten listing to collect search-term data fast.
  • Etsy Stats checked weekly: views, visits, conversion rate and which search terms brought visits.
  • Tags that bring no visits after 30 days are swapped for new phrases from the search-term report.

About this sample: the brand and listing are fictional and were created to show my process and writing on Etsy. No client data or live sales figures are shown. Real before/after results are shared privately on request.

Etsy rules I write to

  • Title: Up to 140 characters; most important words first, written for humans
  • Tags: All 13 tags, 20 characters each, multi-word phrases
  • Attributes: Fill every attribute; they act like extra tags
  • Photos: First photo decides the click on search

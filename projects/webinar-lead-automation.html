Webinar lead automation: round-robin assignment & reminders

n8n Automation Developer

Two n8n workflows that capture webinar sign-ups, assign advisors fairly by round-robin and send automatic reminders, with a Google Sheets tracker that updates itself at every step.

The two n8n workflows.

The challenge

Webinar sign-ups were handled manually, so some leads were missed, advisors got uneven workloads and reminders went out late or not at all, leading to no-shows.

The solution

I built two n8n workflows: one that captures, deduplicates and assigns every lead, and an hourly scheduler that sends reminders at the right time, all tracked in Google Sheets.

How it works

  1. CaptureLeads arrive from the website form.
  2. LogEach lead is written to a Google Sheets tracker.
  3. Check duplicatesPossible duplicates are flagged for manual review.
  4. Assign by round-robinAdvisors are assigned in turn for an even distribution.
  5. Welcome and alertThe lead gets an instant welcome message and the advisor gets an alert.
  6. RemindAn hourly scheduler sends reminders 24 hours and 1 hour before the webinar.

Key features

  • Website form lead capture
  • Self-updating Google Sheets tracker
  • Duplicate flagging with manual review
  • Round-robin advisor assignment
  • Instant welcome message and advisor alert
  • Automatic 24 hour and 1 hour reminders

Results

  • Faster response to every sign-up
  • Even lead distribution across advisors
  • Fewer no-shows thanks to timely reminders

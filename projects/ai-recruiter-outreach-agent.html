The challenge

Recruitment outreach is repetitive: finding contacts, writing a tailored intro for each one, remembering follow-ups and tracking who replied. Done by hand it is slow; fully automated it risks sending the wrong message to the wrong person.

The solution

I built an outreach agent that does the heavy lifting but keeps people in control. AI drafts each email, a recruiter approves, edits or rejects it in Slack, and the system handles sending, follow-ups, replies and reporting.

How it works

  1. ContactsContacts come in from CSV, HubSpot or Salesforce, with a duplicate check.
  2. PersonalizeOpenAI or Gemini writes a unique intro email for each contact.
  3. ApproveThe draft is posted to Slack, where a recruiter approves, edits or rejects it (human gate).
  4. SendApproved emails go out through Gmail, with follow-ups on day 3 and day 7.
  5. Track repliesReplies are read and classified by AI; follow-ups stop on reply or opt-out.
  6. ReportEmails sent, replies and engagement are tracked and reported.

Key features

  • AI-powered personalized email generation
  • Automated Gmail outreach and follow-ups
  • CRM and CSV contact integration
  • AI-based reply classification
  • Duplicate prevention and unsubscribe handling
  • Slack notifications and email approvals
  • Outreach tracking and reporting

Results

  • Recruiters spend time on conversations, not copy and paste
  • Every message is personal, and nothing goes out without approval
  • Follow-ups never get forgotten and stop automatically on reply
  • Clear reporting on outreach activity

Audit: what was holding this listing back

I audit every listing against the same checklist each week. These are the issues I found and how I fixed each one.

ElementIssue foundFix
TitleKeyword-stuffed, “Kegel” and “Balls” crammed in twice, uses “!!” and a disease claim (“Cure Incontinence”) that Amazon and the FDA do not allowBrand first, main keyword once, then set size, material and level. Claim removed
BulletsAll-caps hype, no proof, one bullet makes a medical claimBenefit first, then the fact that supports it. Every claim can be backed by the product spec sheet
Main imageProduct on a pink gradient (fails Amazon’s white-background rule) and too small in framePure white background, set arranged by weight, product fills about 85% of the frame
Secondary imagesThree lifestyle photos, no infographic, no size or weight reference7-image plan below: weights, how-to, materials, case, comparison
A+ contentNone5-module A+ layout with a comparison chart that cross-sells the brand’s other products
Reviews3.9★; top complaint: “not sure which one to start with”Weight guide added to bullet 3, image 4 and the printed insert
Price and coupon$5 more than the two closest competitors with no coupon10% coupon for the test period, then review the margin. See the promotion plan

  1. 1. Main imageFull set on pure white, arranged light to heavy, case slightly behind. No text.
  2. 2. “Start light, get stronger”Infographic of the six weights with grams and a simple level bar.
  3. 3. Which weight is right for me?Three-step start guide that answers the #1 review question.
  4. 4. How to useClean line illustrations, four steps, 10 minutes a day. Tasteful, no explicit imagery.
  5. 5. Materials and careClose-up of silicone texture with icons: phthalate-free, non-porous, easy to clean.
  6. 6. Discreet by designPlain shipping box and zip case shown together.

Promotion plan

  • Weeks 1–4: 10% coupon while the new images and copy collect data, so we can read conversion without a price change.
  • Prime Exclusive Discount in the run-up to Prime Day and Black Friday, when the reference price history qualifies.
  • Lightning Deal or Best Deal only if the margin after fees and ad spend stays positive.
  • Quantity discount for business buyers (pelvic health clinics and physical therapists).
  • No stacking coupon + PED + deal without checking the final price, to protect margin.

Amazon US rules I write to

  • Title: Brand first, no promo words, no word more than twice, category limit checked
  • Bullets: 5 bullets, benefit first, no medical or disease claims
  • Backend terms: Under 250 bytes, no brand names, no repeats
  • Images: Main image on pure white, product fills ~85% of frame

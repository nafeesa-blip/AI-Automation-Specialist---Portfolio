Audit: what was holding this page back

ElementIssue foundFix
SEO titleBrand-only, no product keyword, so the page didn’t rank for “magnesium glycinate”Main keyword + use case + brand, about 60 characters
DescriptionDisease claims (“cures insomnia and anxiety”) break FDA supplement rulesStructure/function wording with the required disclaimer
Above the foldPrice and add to cart pushed below 3 lifestyle banners on mobileImages, price, subscribe option and add to cart in the first screen
TrustReviews hidden in a tab; no testing infoStar rating under the title, “third-party tested” badge with lab link
ImagesNo supplement facts panel imageImage 2 = facts panel, image 3 = capsule size, image 4 = how to take
Structured dataTheme output was missing review dataProduct + AggregateRating schema checked in Google’s Rich Results Test

FAQ added to the page

  • When should I take it? Two capsules about an hour before bed, or as your healthcare provider advises.
  • Why glycinate? It is a gentle form of magnesium that is less likely to upset the stomach than some other forms.
  • Is it tested? Yes, every batch is tested by an independent lab. See the certificate of analysis.
  • Can I cancel my subscription? Any time from your account, with no fees.

Shopify rules I write to

  • SEO title: About 60 characters so Google doesn’t cut it
  • Meta description: About 150–155 characters, one clear benefit + offer
  • Claims: Structure/function claims only, with the FDA disclaimer
  • Alt text: Describe the image; add a keyword only where it fits

Terapeak research

I use Terapeak to see which titles, prices and item specifics sold in the last 90 days, then copy the patterns that win, not the words. For this product, sold listings that named “men & women” and a size range had a higher sell-through than those that did not.

Example finding to show the process; not live data.

Promoted Listings plan

  • General (cost per sale) at a rate close to the category’s suggested ad rate, so we only pay when it sells.
  • Priority (cost per click) on 3–5 keywords that match the title, after the listing is converting well.
  • Weekly check of impressions, click-through rate and sales per listing in Seller Hub.

About this sample: the brand and listing are fictional and were created to show my process and writing on eBay. No client data or live sales figures are shown. Real before/after results are shared privately on request.

eBay rules I write to

  • Title: 80 characters max: brand, product, key specifics, size
  • Item specifics: Fill all required and recommended; they power search filters
  • Description: Mobile-friendly, no active content
  • Policies: Fast handling and free returns help Best Match

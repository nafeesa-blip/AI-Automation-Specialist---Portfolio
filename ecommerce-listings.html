Process

My weekly listing loop

  1. AuditTitle, bullets, A+ content, images, price, coupons and reviews against a fixed checklist.
  2. Find the drop-offSearch Query Performance and analytics show where shoppers leave: search, click, cart or checkout.
  3. Rewrite and briefNew copy mapped to keywords, and a clear brief for the designer for each image.
  4. Align with adsThe PPC team targets the same keywords the listing is built around.
  5. TestOne change at a time (Manage Your Experiments on Amazon), run until the result is clear.
  6. Watch the marketCompetitor price, coupons, images, titles and new launches, with a suggested response.
  7. Learn from reviewsComplaints become listing fixes and product ideas.
